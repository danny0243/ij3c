International Journal of Computer, Consumer and Control (IJ3C)

A peer-reviewed international journal for research in computing, consumer electronics, control, signal processing, communications, renewable energy, and related engineering technologies.

Browse Issues Author Guide

Welcome to IJ3C

Rapid developments in Computer, Consumer and Control technologies for intelligent living and green energy have made 3C theory and signal processing a valuable area of research and design. IJ3C provides an outlet for significant research papers and state-of-the-art review articles in these fields.

The journal welcomes original high-quality papers related to advanced multimedia, computer technologies, telecommunications, semiconductors, consumer electronics, renewable energy, systems and control, and digital signal processing. The journal is published four times annually.

Referee Policy Peer review processing Evaluation by multiple scholars or experienced experts Final decision within six months

Author Guide & Submission

Manuscript preparation

Please ensure that your manuscript satisfies originality, relevance, motivation, referencing, clarity, and length requirements before submission. Original papers should be submitted as a single PDF file that includes all figures and tables. Accepted papers should be submitted in source file format.

Manuscripts must be written in English and should generally not exceed 10,000 words. Papers must be typed in Times New Roman, no smaller than 10 pt, in two-column format on A4 paper.
